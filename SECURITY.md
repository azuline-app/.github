# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Report it privately in one of two ways:

- On GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
- By email to security@azuline.app.

Say what is affected (desktop app, browser extension, server or website), which version, how to reproduce the
problem, and what an attacker could do with it.

## What happens next

azuline is maintained by one person. You'll get a reply within 7 days. We agree a disclosure date with you,
normally when the fix is released and no later than 90 days after your report. You're credited in the release
notes unless you'd rather not be named.

There's no bug bounty, because azuline is free and has no budget for one.

## Scope

- The desktop app and its background agent, including the local connection it opens for the browser extension
  and plugins.
- The browser extension and the plugins for drawing apps.
- The sync server and, once it exists, the hosted service at azuline.app.
- Release signing and the update channel.

## Supported versions

Nothing has been released yet. After the first release, security fixes go to the newest version in each release
channel.

## Research in good faith

We won't take legal action over research done in good faith that respects other people's privacy and data, causes
no damage, and gives us time to release a fix before the details are published.
