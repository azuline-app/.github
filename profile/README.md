azuline is a free, open-source app for artists. It's in early development, so there's nothing to download yet.

## What we promise

- **We will never add generative AI to azuline.** It never generates, edits, upscales or completes artwork.
- **We don't train on your art.** Nothing in your library is used to train any model, and we never sell or
  license anyone's data.
- **Files stay on your computer.** Images leave it only when you share something or turn on sync, and then only
  to the place you picked.

## Get in touch

- Bugs and ideas: open an issue in the repository the problem belongs to.
- Security problems: report them privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/azuline-app/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- News and updates: [@azulineapp](https://x.com/azulineapp) on X.
- Anything else: hello@azuline.app
