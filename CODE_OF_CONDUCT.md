# Code of conduct

The people around azuline come from many countries and languages, and from every level of drawing and coding
experience. This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and every community channel the project runs.

## Expected

- Be patient with beginners. Questions about how something works are always welcome.
- Comment on the work, never on the person.
- Credit other people's art and code whenever you share it.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or attacks on anyone's identity, art or skill.
- Posting other people's art without credit, or sharing private information about anyone.
- Sexual content involving minors, or characters presented as minors, in any form.
- Spam, and advertising unrelated to azuline.
- Pushing again and again for features the project has ruled out, such as generative AI.

## Reporting

Email hello@azuline.app with "Conduct" in the subject. Every report is read, and the reporter's name stays
private. The maintainer may remove content, give a warning, or ban someone from the project's spaces, depending on
what happened.
